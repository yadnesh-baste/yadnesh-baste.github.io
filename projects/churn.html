Who leaves a bank, and whom to call first

One in five customers of this European retail bank left. I found where churn concentrates, built a model that ranks customers by risk, and turned it into a call list a retention team can work through from the top.

What stands out

Age, not tenure. Customers aged 50 to 59 leave at 56%, nearly three times the average. Tenure barely matters.

The product count is not linear. Customers with two products are the most loyal (8% churn). One product means 28%, and three or more means 86%. That last group is small, but it suggests cross-selling that went wrong rather than deepening relationships.

Germany is different. German customers leave at 32%, twice the rate in France and Spain, even though they hold higher balances. That's a question for the German business, not only for the model.

Inactive customers leave twice as often as active ones (27% vs 14%), which makes activity a cheap early-warning signal.

The model

I compared logistic regression with LightGBM on a held-out 30% of customers. LightGBM reaches an ROC-AUC of (logistic regression: ; 5-fold cross-validation: ), because it captures the non-linear effects of age and product count. The strongest drivers are age, number of products and balance. Estimated salary ranks high too, but it shows no pattern on its own, so I treat it as noise rather than a business insight.

What I'd recommend

Work the call list from the top: with capacity for a fifth of customers, the team reaches two thirds of leavers. Pair each call with the reason the segment points to: re-activation offers for inactive customers, a product review for customers with three or more products, and a separate look at German customers. Then measure retention against a randomly held-back control group, so the effect of the calls can be proven rather than assumed.

This mirrors my work at Bajaj Finance, where churn and drop-off analysis with targeted retention initiatives raised customer retention from 15% to 50%. The data here is public, the method is the same.

Power BI version

The repository includes the data model and the DAX measures to rebuild this dashboard in Power BI: churn rate, leavers, and the share of leavers reached at a chosen call capacity.
